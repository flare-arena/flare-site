The idea

Talent is everywhere. Stages are not.

Bengaluru is full of players who never get a proper arena: the student who is the best in her college, the academy athlete between selections, the colleague who plays like a pro after six. FLARE is the arena. Properly run. Properly seen. Built here, and built to travel.

FAQ

Good
questions.

Who can take part?

Schools and colleges, sports institutions such as academies and clubs, and corporate teams in Bengaluru. Each edition is either schools or colleges, never both, so competition stays fair.

Which sports are on the programme?

Badminton, football, basketball, table tennis and cricket. Final sports and categories are confirmed with entries, and we're open to adjusting as we learn.

Where will matches be played?

At a single host venue in Bengaluru, so every sport shares one tournament atmosphere. The venue will be announced as the first season is confirmed.

How long does a season run?

Each edition is planned as a structured 30 to 45 day season, with published fixtures from the first match to the final.

What about corporate teams?

Corporate competitions run separately from institution tournaments, at their own venue. Sports and formats will be announced as FLARE grows.

How do I register or partner with FLARE?

Registrations and partnership enquiries open soon. Follow FLARE on Instagram and LinkedIn to be first to know.
